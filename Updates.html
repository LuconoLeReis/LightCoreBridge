WayBeyond20 1.73.57

Stable release · November 1, 2026 · Browser build 2.20.189.

Consistent action columns

The existing Name, Range, Hit/DC, Damage and Notes columns stay in place. Added ranges follow native number/unit/Reach/Touch styling. Clickable values have native-style borders and known damage icons; Hit/DC matches damage text size. Identical equipped weapons appear once, while different statistics and properties stay separate. Spell notes keep useful details and source-confirmed material prices without repeating action costs. Inventory and existing execution paths are retained.

VTT delivery and local fallback

Automatic sends the same completed result to available VTTs. Confirmed delivery suppresses the extra WB20 local result; otherwise the completed result falls back locally, subject to existing display/privacy settings. Optional delivery never holds up action completion or repeats spending. Saved destination readiness is bounded, current permitted Foundry tabs are rediscovered, and native spell shortcuts use spell casting controls.

Temporary local results and simpler settings

Local results use a centered, nonmodal panel that fades after five seconds by default. Change Duration of Local Rolls display under Display and privacy. Hover or focus pauses fading; Close, Escape and outside clicks dismiss it. Settings opens in a normal tab. Use Beyond20 mode remains in Settings and the extension menu; the permanent page button is removed. The readable saved TaleSpire connection is reused across boards. Header icon ink and spacing follow the selected sheet.

Reliability repairs

Shared-sheet links load correctly, filtered or incomplete Features views retain still-owned rules, and the action-list collector no longer deletes another feature module's rows. Refresh work combines mutation bursts and reuses rule reads within a render. The Defenses/Buffs frame accommodates its content. These address reproduced problems, not every possible loading cause.

After updating

Finish pending rolls and close D&D Beyond/VTT tabs. Back up the loaded extension folder, replace its contents in place from the Chrome ZIP, reload WayBeyond20 on chrome://extensions, then reopen your sheets and VTTs. Keep the existing loaded path rather than removing/reinstalling. Old tabs can retain stale scripts. Intermittent X/Stop-loading behavior remains under investigation; fresh tabs are the current workaround.

Verified scope and known limits

Automated suites, focused browser/recovery checks, both builds and independent review passed. Fresh Calan/Rhonen load/display was accepted. Bounded live tests covered attack/damage prompts, one slot spend and restoration, and authenticated TaleSpire receipts; native TaleSpire chat was not separately screenshotted. Full live Forge and every-class acceptance are not claimed.

Native digital-dice audience warnings can accompany fallback dice while play continues. The existing digital-dice hide setting can suppress a local card even without delivery. Old companions or late acknowledgments may leave an extra local copy. Native enemy HP and terrain/path adjudication are not synchronized.
