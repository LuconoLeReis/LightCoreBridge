WayBeyond20 1.73.57

Browser build 2.20.189.

Consistent action columns

Your current five-column list stays in place. Added ranges, roll values and native damage icons match the sheet more closely. Identical equipped weapons appear once; different statistics and properties remain separate. Spell notes keep useful details without repeating the left border's action cost, and can indicate source-confirmed material prices.

Temporary local results

Local results return to a centered nonmodal panel and fade after five seconds by default. Adjust Duration of Local Rolls display in Display and privacy. Hover or focus pauses fading. Close, Escape and outside clicks dismiss the panel.

Delivery and recovery

Automatic sends the completed result to available VTTs and uses local display when delivery cannot be confirmed. Rolls and actions do not wait for a destination. Recovery remains in Settings and the extension menu; its permanent page button has been removed.

After updating

Finish pending rolls, close D&D Beyond and VTT tabs, replace files in your existing loaded extension folder, reload the extension, and reopen the tabs. Old injected scripts remain until refreshed or reopened. Intermittent loading after updates remains under investigation; a fresh tab is the current workaround.

See README.txt and the source changelog for installation and known limits. No claim is made that every class, rules combination or VTT has full live acceptance.
