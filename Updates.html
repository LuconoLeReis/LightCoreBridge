LightCore Bridge 1.73.72

The first LightCore Bridge release. Previously WayBeyond20; your saved settings, character data and connections are retained. Browser build 2.20.204.

Your character, connected to your table

Play locally with D&D Beyond or deliver completed rolls to supported VTTs. A VTT is not required. Existing integration identities, licenses and Beyond20 attribution stay intact.

Clearer Actions and corrections

Consistent names, subtitles, selected spell levels, ranges, bordered values and damage indicators. Notes give concise reminders and open full rules. Opportunity Attack remembers a specific weapon or qualifying War Caster spell. Portent availability can be restored in Correction without rerolling its value.

Active companions

Find Steed and Summon Undead use the selected cast level, creature type and spellcasting source. Creature actions use the existing attack system and do not borrow the caster's personal rerolls or resources. Animate Dead supports separate creatures, creation, renewal, HP, control hours and a group command.

Open the desired Skeleton/Zombie reference in Extras once to cache its native definition. Track an existing summon adds controls without another cast or slot. Missing definitions preserve Continue without tracking. Manual renewal now survives normal spell-sidebar dismissal while retaining creature identity and HP.

Companion limits

Control hours are manually advanced game time. Multiattack shows the count; each click rolls one attack. Initiative, position, aura eligibility, target conditions and native companion HP synchronization are not automated. Full rules remain available for table adjudication. The separate experimental mastery/rider branch is not included.

Verification

Independent offline review, automated regression suites, companion composition tests, syntax checks and Chrome/Firefox builds passed. Live renewal and actual Animate Dead/Summon Undead casts verified retained HP/identity, committed effects and exactly one slot per cast; test resources were restored. This does not claim live acceptance of every class, homebrew feature or VTT.

Update in place

Finish pending rolls and close character/VTT tabs. Back up the loaded extension folder, replace its contents from the Chrome ZIP, reload LightCore Bridge on chrome://extensions, then reopen your sheets. Preserve the same loaded path and installation. Old tabs can retain stale scripts.

Intermittent loading indicators remain under investigation. Digital-dice privacy settings can suppress a local result even without delivery; older companions or late acknowledgments can produce an extra local copy.
