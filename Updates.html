LightCore Bridge updates

Recent changes

  • Jack of All Trades and Initiative. The existing setting includes the requested2014-style Initiative treatment. A final check preserves proficiency already included and prevents repeated processing from adding twice. Other ability checks are unchanged. Unlabelled extra bonuses require confirmation, remembered for the unchanged calculation. Cancel sends no roll.
  • Spell slot accounting. Slot-funded spells cast with the LightCore roll icon now spend one slot at the selected casting level after successful dispatch, including Chromatic Orb. This includes spell-list to-hit dice and Actions-tab spell attack dice; do not also mark the slot pip by hand. Canceled casts, failed dispatches and separate damage rolls do not spend a slot. Unverified payment warns without casting again.
  • TaleSpire Combat Window. A paused board feed preserves manually opened combat. To adopt native initiative, enable Follow this board's turn order in the companion; seeing minis alone does not enable following.
  • Immediate results. Attack-only criticals retain their modifier in the Game Log. Standalone healing and save-only casts no longer wait for an unrelated attack summary.
  • Keep Roll. Decline a Protected reroll without spending Luck Points by choosing Keep Roll.
  • Attack damage. In manual damage mode, the Roll Damages control stays available on the sheet while damage is pending, including when local dice results are hidden. An unsuccessful resource-history save no longer stops an attack after its hit roll.
  • D&D Beyond Game Log. Named rolls carry the action name. With Digital Dice and Game Log enabled, a completed managed hit shows the damage total and its typed breakdown together. Conditional or second-target damage is separate from the main total.
  • Combat Tracker. The tracker shows your Initiative total. Open Correction to enter or adjust it. In manual combat, the tracker offers to advance your turn after you finish your actions, bonus actions and attacks.
  • Protected Luck. On Advantage or Super Advantage, the reroll prompt appears only if all eligible natural d20 results are 9 or lower.

Your character, connected to your table

Play locally with D&D Beyond or deliver completed rolls to supported VTTs. A VTT is not required. Existing integration identities, licenses and Beyond20 attribution stay intact.

Clearer Actions and corrections

Consistent names, subtitles, selected spell levels, ranges, bordered values and damage indicators. Notes give concise reminders and open full rules. Opportunity Attack remembers a specific weapon or qualifying War Caster spell. Portent availability can be restored in Correction without rerolling its value.

Active companions

Find Steed and Summon Undead use the selected cast level, creature type and spellcasting source. Creature actions use the existing attack system and do not borrow the caster's personal rerolls or resources. Animate Dead supports separate creatures, creation, renewal, HP, control hours and a group command.

Open the desired Skeleton/Zombie reference in Extras once to cache its native definition. Track an existing summon adds controls without another cast or slot. Missing definitions preserve Continue without tracking. Manual renewal now survives normal spell-sidebar dismissal while retaining creature identity and HP.

Companion limits

Control hours are manually advanced game time. Multiattack shows the count; each click rolls one attack. Initiative, position, aura eligibility, target conditions and native companion HP synchronization are not automated. Full rules remain available for table adjudication. The separate experimental mastery/rider branch is not included.

Release scope

112 adds an Initiative-only final proficiency guard to111; other Jack-of-All-Trades paths are unchanged. 42 focused cases, the full automated suite and independent offline code review passed; no new installed gameplay acceptance is claimed. Experimental session-sync code is not included. Free native spells labelled Cast instead of Use and Pact Magic pool selection remain unverified on real sheets.

Update in place

Finish pending rolls and close character and VTT tabs. Back up the folder Chrome loads, then replace its contents with the new build. If Dropbox supplies the build, wait for it to finish syncing. Reload LightCore Bridge on chrome://extensions, then reopen your sheets. Keep the same folder and installation to retain your saved settings.
